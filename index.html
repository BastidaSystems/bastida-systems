AI Agents · Installation

We install AI agents — and leave them connected

We don't sell the agent — we sell the installation. We install agents like Muse or Codex inside your company and leave them connected — to your website, your business Gmail or your computer — ready to help with day-to-day operations and administrative control, with supervision. It is the same installation we run ourselves: our own agent is already connected to all of our systems.

Connected to: your website · your business Gmail · your computer

Muse — for businesses

Our main installation for companies: we set up Muse and connect it to your tools and data (for example, your database in Supabase) — queries, records, reports and follow-ups on your real information, under your rules.

Codex — for game studios & developers

For video game companies and new developers: we install Codex and connect it to your code and deployments (GitHub and Vercel) — builds and changes go live in minutes, with review before publishing.

Connections we already run

Already working in production: GitHub, Vercel, Supabase, Stripe, Google Analytics & Search Console.
