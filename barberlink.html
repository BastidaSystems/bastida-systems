Booked in under a minute

The house rule: reserving takes less than a minute with just your name and a phone or email. An account is optional — Apple or Google — and earns points and favorites.

Your barber, your time

Choose the shop, the professional and the service — fades, classic cuts, beard work — with the price shown before you confirm. No surprises at the chair.

Reviews you can trust

Only clients with a completed booking can leave a review, and the shop calendar blocks double bookings automatically.
